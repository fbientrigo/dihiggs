## What is established

[DERIVED][PROJECT-DEFINITION] Starting from the Type-I Yukawa Lagrangian with only `Phi2`, all charged-fermion masses are generated by `v2=v s_beta`:

\[
\boxed{m_f=\frac{y_f v_2}{\sqrt2}=\frac{y_f v s_\beta}{\sqrt2}}.
\]

[DERIVED] The CP-even interaction in the fermion mass basis is

\[
\boxed{\mathcal L_Y^{\rm CP-even}=-\sum_f\frac{m_f}{v s_\beta}\rho_2\,\bar f f.}
\]

Using the inverse Phase-4 rotation

\[
\boxed{\rho_2=c_\alpha h+s_\alpha\phi},
\]

the exact tree-level modifiers relative to the SM scalar Yukawa normalization `-(m_f/v) S \bar f f` are

\[
\boxed{\kappa_f^h=\frac{c_\alpha}{s_\beta}},
\qquad
\boxed{\kappa_f^\phi=\frac{s_\alpha}{s_\beta}},
\qquad f=u,d,\ell.
\]

Equivalently,

\[
\boxed{\kappa_f^h=s_{\beta-\alpha}+c_{\beta-\alpha}\cot\beta},
\]

\[
\boxed{\kappa_f^\phi=c_{\beta-\alpha}-s_{\beta-\alpha}\cot\beta}.
\]

Therefore on the project exact-alignment branch

\[
s_{\beta-\alpha}=+1,\qquad c_{\beta-\alpha}=0,
\]

we obtain

\[
\boxed{\kappa_f^h=1},
\qquad
\boxed{\kappa_f^\phi=-\cot\beta}
\]

for up quarks, down quarks, and charged leptons.

Thus claim C3 is promoted to **VERIFIED** in the frozen Phase-4 field convention.

The sign is not inferred from a source coupling table. It follows from the already-fixed field identity

\[
\phi=s_\beta\rho_1-c_\beta\rho_2
\]

at exact alignment, which implies

\[
\rho_2=\cdots-c_\beta\phi,
\]

and from the fact that `Phi2` alone generates fermion masses.

## Derivation

### 1. Begin from gauge-invariant Yukawa operators

For one generation, suppressing flavour indices, the project Type-I Yukawa Lagrangian is

\[
\boxed{
-\mathcal L_Y=
 y_d\,\bar Q_L\Phi_2 d_R
+y_u\,\bar Q_L\widetilde\Phi_2 u_R
+y_\ell\,\bar L_L\Phi_2\ell_R
+\mathrm{h.c.}
}
\]

with

\[
\widetilde\Phi_2\equiv i\sigma_2\Phi_2^*.
\]

For three generations, `y_f` becomes a matrix. Natural flavour conservation in Type I means that only one Yukawa matrix per fermion charge exists. After the standard bi-unitary rotations into the fermion mass basis, the neutral CP-even couplings are diagonal and the following one-generation algebra applies eigenvalue by eigenvalue.

### 2. Expand the neutral component of `Phi2`

From Phase 1,

\[
\Phi_2=
\begin{pmatrix}
\phi_2^+\\[1mm]
\dfrac{v_2+\rho_2+i\eta_2}{\sqrt2}
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Therefore

\[
\widetilde\Phi_2=
\begin{pmatrix}
\dfrac{v_2+\rho_2-i\eta_2}{\sqrt2}\\[1mm]
-\phi_2^-
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

The **CP-even** neutral factor is the same for up, down, and charged-lepton Yukawas:

\[
\frac{v_2+\rho_2}{\sqrt2}.
\]

Consequently,

\[
-\mathcal L_Y\supset
\frac{y_f}{\sqrt2}(v_2+\rho_2)\bar f f
\]

for each charged fermion after adding the Hermitian conjugate and choosing real positive mass eigenvalues.

### 3. Separate the mass term from the interaction

The VEV term gives

\[
-\mathcal L_Y\supset \frac{y_fv_2}{\sqrt2}\bar f f.
\]

Comparing with the standard Dirac mass term

\[
\mathcal L_{\rm mass}=-m_f\bar f f
\]

gives

\[
\boxed{m_f=\frac{y_fv_2}{\sqrt2}}.
\]

Hence

\[
y_f=\frac{\sqrt2m_f}{v_2}
=\frac{\sqrt2m_f}{v s_\beta}.
\]

The CP-even interaction is therefore

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal L_Y^{\rm CP-even}
=-\frac{m_f}{v_2}\rho_2\bar f f
=-\frac{m_f}{v s_\beta}\rho_2\bar f f.
}
\]

This step fixes the overall sign with no reference to any coupling table.

### 4. Invert the Phase-4 CP-even rotation

Phase 4 froze

\[
\begin{pmatrix}h\\\phi\end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix}-s_\alpha&c_\alpha\\c_\alpha&s_\alpha\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}\rho_1\\\rho_2\end{pmatrix}.
\]

The matrix is orthogonal and symmetric, so its inverse is itself:

\[
\begin{pmatrix}\rho_1\\\rho_2\end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix}-s_\alpha&c_\alpha\\c_\alpha&s_\alpha\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}h\\\phi\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Thus

\[
\boxed{\rho_1=-s_\alpha h+c_\alpha\phi},
\qquad
\boxed{\rho_2=c_\alpha h+s_\alpha\phi}.
\]

Insert the second expression into the Yukawa interaction:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal L_Y^{\rm CP-even}
&=-\frac{m_f}{v s_\beta}
(c_\alpha h+s_\alpha\phi)\bar f f\\
&=-\frac{m_f}{v}
\left[
\frac{c_\alpha}{s_\beta}h
+\frac{s_\alpha}{s_\beta}\phi
\right]\bar f f.
\end{aligned}
\]

Define the modifiers by

\[
\mathcal L_Y^{\rm CP-even}
\equiv-\frac{m_f}{v}
\left(\kappa_f^h h+\kappa_f^\phi\phi\right)\bar f f.
\]

Coefficient matching then yields

\[
\boxed{\kappa_f^h=\frac{c_\alpha}{s_\beta}},
\qquad
\boxed{\kappa_f^\phi=\frac{s_\alpha}{s_\beta}}.
\]

No mass ordering and no source naming convention has entered this derivation.

### 5. Rewrite in terms of `beta-alpha`

Use

\[
\alpha=\beta-(\beta-\alpha).
\]

Then

\[
\cos\alpha
=\cos\beta\cos(\beta-\alpha)
+\sin\beta\sin(\beta-\alpha),
\]

so

\[
\frac{c_\alpha}{s_\beta}
=s_{\beta-\alpha}+c_{\beta-\alpha}\cot\beta.
\]

Similarly,

\[
\sin\alpha
=\sin\beta\cos(\beta-\alpha)
-\cos\beta\sin(\beta-\alpha),
\]

hence

\[
\frac{s_\alpha}{s_\beta}
=c_{\beta-\alpha}-s_{\beta-\alpha}\cot\beta.
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{\kappa_f^h=s_{\beta-\alpha}+c_{\beta-\alpha}\cot\beta},
\]

\[
\boxed{\kappa_f^\phi=c_{\beta-\alpha}-s_{\beta-\alpha}\cot\beta}.
\]

These identities are exact at tree level in the selected Type-I basis.

### 6. Exact alignment

For the project campaign convention

\[
s_{\beta-\alpha}=+1,
\qquad c_{\beta-\alpha}=0,
\]

we get immediately

\[
\boxed{\kappa_f^h=1},
\]

\[
\boxed{\kappa_f^\phi=-\cot\beta}.
\]

The same result can be seen directly from the aligned field directions. Phase 4 found

\[
h=c_\beta\rho_1+s_\beta\rho_2,
\qquad
\phi=s_\beta\rho_1-c_\beta\rho_2.
\]

Inverting,

\[
\rho_2=s_\beta h-c_\beta\phi.
\]

Hence

\[
\mathcal L_Y^{\rm CP-even}
=-\frac{m_f}{v s_\beta}(s_\beta h-c_\beta\phi)\bar f f
=-\frac{m_f}{v}\left(h-\cot\beta\,\phi\right)\bar f f.
\]

So the coefficient multiplying `phi` inside the modifier bracket is unambiguously `-cot(beta)`.

### 7. CP-odd Yukawas: useful secondary derivation

Phase 4 fixed

\[
G^0=c_\beta\eta_1+s_\beta\eta_2,
\qquad
A=-s_\beta\eta_1+c_\beta\eta_2,
\]

therefore

\[
\eta_2=s_\beta G^0+c_\beta A.
\]

For down quarks and charged leptons, the neutral field appears as

\[
\Phi_2^0=\frac{v_2+\rho_2+i\eta_2}{\sqrt2}.
\]

After adding the Hermitian conjugate,

\[
\mathcal L_Y\supset
-i\frac{m_f}{v_2}\eta_2\bar f\gamma_5 f,
\qquad f=d,\ell.
\]

For up quarks, `tilde Phi2` contains the opposite imaginary sign,

\[
(\widetilde\Phi_2)^0
=\frac{v_2+\rho_2-i\eta_2}{\sqrt2},
\]

so

\[
\mathcal L_Y\supset
+i\frac{m_u}{v_2}\eta_2\bar u\gamma_5u.
\]

Dropping the Goldstone part and using `eta2 -> c_beta A`,

\[
\mathcal L_A
=+i\frac{m_u}{v}\cot\beta\,A\bar u\gamma_5u
-i\frac{m_d}{v}\cot\beta\,A\bar d\gamma_5d
-i\frac{m_\ell}{v}\cot\beta\,A\bar\ell\gamma_5\ell.
\]

If one adopts the BFLRS11 definition

\[
\mathcal L_Y\supset
+ i\frac{m_f}{v}\xi_A^f A\bar f\gamma_5f,
\]

then

\[
\boxed{\xi_A^u=+\cot\beta,\qquad
\xi_A^d=\xi_A^\ell=-\cot\beta}.
\]

This CP-odd result is not required for C3 but provides an internal sign check of the `tilde Phi2` convention.

### 8. Consequence for tree-level fermionic widths

For a CP-even scalar `S` with

\[
\mathcal L\supset-\frac{m_f}{v}\kappa_f^S S\bar f f,
\]

the tree-level two-body width is

\[
\Gamma(S\to f\bar f)
=N_c\frac{m_S}{8\pi}\frac{m_f^2}{v^2}
|\kappa_f^S|^2
\left(1-\frac{4m_f^2}{m_S^2}\right)^{3/2}.
\]

Thus at fixed `m_phi`, fermion mass prescription, and perturbative corrections,

\[
\boxed{\Gamma(\phi\to f\bar f)\propto\cot^2\beta}
\]

in exact alignment.

This is a **conditional scaling statement**, not a statement that the total width or lifetime globally scales as `cot^2 beta`: other open channels and loop/scalar couplings can have different parameter dependence.

## What was checked against the source

1. BFLRS11 source lines 530–545 states that in Type I all quarks couple to one doublet, conventionally `Phi2`; lines 633–652 show `u_R,d_R,e_R -> Phi2` in the natural-flavour-conservation table.
2. BFLRS11 Eq. `Eq:Yukawa`, source lines 710–766, defines the Yukawa modifiers relative to `-(m_f/v)` and lists Type-I CP-even coefficients `cos(alpha)/sin(beta)` and `sin(alpha)/sin(beta)`.
3. The BFLRS11 early displayed CP-even fields have the global sign issue already recorded in the convention ledger. They are therefore **not** used to derive the project sign.
4. GHOO18 source lines 1960–1969 gives the general weak-basis Yukawa Lagrangian. Its Type-I section, lines 2092–2118, sets `eta1^{u,d,l}=0`, hence only `Phi2` couples, and obtains `rho^f \propto cot(beta)`.
5. GHOO18 AL lines 2123–2136 show the CP-even non-SM state coupling with magnitude `cot(beta)`; after applying the Phase-4 field map `H2_GHOO=-phi_project`, the sign agrees with the independent project derivation.
6. DH05 is retained as a convention bridge only. Its Type-I condition is basis-invariant, and its text explicitly discusses a special basis choice; swapping `Phi1 <-> Phi2` maps its alternative Type-I assignment to the project one.

### Independent implementation check against active 2HDMC

Only after the analytic derivation was frozen, the active vendored implementation was checked. `THDM::set_yukawas_type` states that it uses the hep-ph/0504050 convention and for `type==1` scales all three Higgs-basis Yukawa matrices as

```cpp
rho_D *= 1./tan(beta);
rho_U *= 1./tan(beta);
rho_L *= 1./tan(beta);
```

so the non-SM Yukawa structure has common Type-I magnitude `cot(beta)`. Combined with the DH/project Phase-4 scalar sign, its exact-alignment CP-even coupling is `-cot(beta)`, matching the independent derivation.

This implementation evidence is `[IMPLEMENTATION-CHECKED]`; it did not determine the analytic result.
